## Access and stopping decisions

Some initial PDF URLs returned errors in the web reader. Existing local original PDFs supplied those texts where available; the Admoni full text was retrieved through its CMU alternate URL. A sandboxed direct-download attempt failed because DNS/network access was unavailable; it did not change the evidence status or create a downloaded source. Web retrieval supplied the Sun and Mirror Eyes author texts instead.

Wang and Shiwa remain abstract-level in this review. Their claims are restricted to what the original abstract states. GazeGrasp was considered from the supplied dossier but excluded from core evidence because it concerns industrial grasping rather than visual communication. The capstone-library article, student anecdotes, submission routes, and publisher-policy material were reviewed as task context; they do not answer the technical question and are excluded from the paper's references.

The search stopped when the supplied themes had identifiable primary passages, contrasting findings, adjacent reviews, and explicit transfer limits sufficient for a focused narrative synthesis. This stopping rule supports bounded coverage, not exhaustiveness. Deduplication used DOI and title; author and published versions of the same work were not counted as separate studies.
